# hpi-information-systems_metanome

Metanome was really active from 2013 to 2015 and then declined a lot. The longest gap was 13 months (2024-01 to 2025-01). It was easy to interpret because the commits before the gap were almost all dependabot, so nobody was really working on it. It came back a little in 2025 when a new contributor added a feature, but then went back to mostly bot commits.
