# brownhci_drafty

Drafty's activity was irregular, with a quiet period in 2018 and then big spikes from 2019 to 2022. The longest gap was 9 months in 2018. This one was easy to interpret because right after the gap there's a commit called 'Old Drafty final commit' and then the repo gets cleaned out and restarted. So the gap was between the old version and a rewrite, and new people joined for the rewrite.
